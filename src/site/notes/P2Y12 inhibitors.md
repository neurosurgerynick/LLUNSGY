Clopidogrel (Plavix) and Prasugrel are processed by the liver into active metabolites that **irreversibly** inhibit platelet aggregation

Aspirin inhibits COX1 from the arachadonic acid pathway and prevents platelet aggregation by decreased production of TXA2 (thromboxane A2)
	Thromboxane A2 activates Thromboxane Prostaglanin (TP) receptors which are GPCRs (Gq) receptors, increased intracellular Ca and activation of Protein Kinase C (PKC)
	

Ticagrelor and Cangrelor are IV medications used in the endovascular angio suite that can be loaded and are reversible inhibitors of P2Y12
