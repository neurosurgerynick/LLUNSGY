## Materials
- Antibiotic-impregnated drainage catheter
- 3x 3-0 nylon/ethilon sutures
- many extra flushes
- Cranial access kit and hermetic drainage system
- Lidocaine with epinephrine for hemostasis
- CHG tegaderm dressing 
- Steri-strips

## Patient positioning
Supine, head elevated 30 degrees, head at the top of the bed
## Insertion technique
Identify Kocher's point 10.5 cm posterior to nasion and 3 cm lateral in the mid-pupillary line
Make a wide vertical linear incision and spread the skin with the grasshopper handle facing anteriorly
Drill with a cranial drill, ensure that the drill is locked into the handle
Tunnel the EVD tunneler 5 cm posteriorly and protect it
pierce the dura with an 18g needle used to draw up lidocaine
pass the EVD catheter into the frontal horn of the lateral ventricle about 6-7cm at the bone, confirm csf flow
Attach the EVD catheter to the tunneler
Pass 2 3-0 nylon vertical mattress sutures around the tunneler for high-risk cases of CSF leak
Pass the EVD catheter using the tunneler

## EVD care bundle
- antibiotic catheter
- Neurosurgical cart should have a full body drape
- Chlorhexidine eluting strip
- Occlusive CHG tegaderm dressing
	- Perimeter of steri strips

## EVD risks
2% risk of major intracranial hemorrhage
