### Wada test
used for dominance of language and memory for seizure surgery (temporal lobectomy)
done with neurology showing them items 
patient will slur speech and drop the contralateral arm when the motor cortex is hit
amobarbital (truth serum) is infused into each carotid while patient is awake, to temporarily knock out the function of the cerebral hemisphere
if you hit the posterior fossa, the patient will pass out from amobarbital in the RAS
ask them questions if they can remember or speak
