Non-endometrial location of implantation
- most frequently in the fallopian tube (95% with the ampulla being the most common)
B-hcg ==doubles every 48 hours== in a **normal pregnancy**
	However, for an **ectopic pregnancy** the b-hcg lags behind and is often lower than expected for gestational age
Transvaginal US should detect an intrauterine pregnancy by **5 weeks** in a normal pregnancy (b-hcg 1000-2000)

## Treatment
-methotrexate
- contraindicated for:
	-  hepatic or renal disease (Type 1 diabetes with nephropathy)
	- immunodeficiency
	- peptic ulcer disease
	- breastfeeding
