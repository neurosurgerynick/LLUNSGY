## Kawase Approach
Management of aneurysms arisng from the vertebrobasilar junction
Solution: transpetrosal approach invented by Dr. Kawase
Indications: Lesions located in the middle fossa, upper/mid clivus
Positioning:
	supine
	head turned 
	tilted 20 degrees toward the floor, so zygoma is highest point
	Ipsilateral shoulder roll

The root of the zygoma takes you right down to foramen ovale (containing V3)
Posterior portion of the craniotomy should be 2/3 and the anterior portion should be 1/3 

Flattening the floor of the middle fossa
First structure you see is the MMA, you can follow down to the foramen spinosum
Divide the MMA
![Pasted image 20260128082911.png](/img/user/assets/Pasted%20image%2020260128082911.png)
notce that the kawase area is medial to GSPN which heads posteriorly to the Geniculate ganglion on its way just lateral to the cochlea.
![[Pasted image 20260128090015.png \| 500]]

The carotid is right under the GSPN. Posteromedial to the cochlea is the labyrinth whose superior circular canal is contained by the arcuate eminence. Note that the 4th nerve travels back along the tentorial edge. You need to cut the tentorium from the medial area, noting that the trochelar nerve lies just behind the tentorial edge. You open the dura into the posterior fossa.
