Mild symptomatic carotid stenosis
	Defined by <50% narrowing, current guideline AHA is 50% for intervention
Asymptomatic carotid stenosis
	Guideline for intervention according to AHA is >70%
NASCET criteria: CTA can underestimate narrowing, MRA can **overestimate** narrowing due to flow related artifact (more prone to false positives)
	Usually carotid narrowing happens at the carotid bifurcation, note that the carotid bulb has a natural dilatation AND that there exist post-stenotic dilatation
	Gold standard: angiography
