## Presurgical conference
T2 imaging standing waves, caused by aneurysmal pulsations. Used to differentiate tumor from a large aneurysm


Case of a female with a giant Cavernous segment aneurysm, and persistent trigeminal artery (PTA)
### Persistent trigeminal artery
![Pasted image 20251029082446.png](/img/user/assets/Pasted%20image%2020251029082446.png)
PTA is the most common of the embryologic remnants; the persistent trigeminal artery travels from the ICA to supply the basilar artery. 

![Pasted image 20251029082907.png](/img/user/assets/Pasted%20image%2020251029082907.png)
Recall the dural rings to determine intradural and extradural origin. If an aneurysm comes off the cavernous segment, it is most likely extradural. Some cavernous segment aneurysms don't even need to be followed because they do not cause SAH, as they are extradural. Instead, when they rupture, they cause [[CCF; CC Fistulas\|CCF; CC Fistulas]]

## Treatment of Giant cerebral aneurysms with endovascular approach
Most effective is flow diversion with few large coils in an aneurysm, dual microcatheter technique
Most aneurysms >1 cm you need to pack with coils because flow diversion is usually not enough to shut down the aneurysm
Preload patients with cangrelor (IV; short acting; good for loading in the angio suite) and then continue Prasugrel after flow diversion [[P2Y12 inhibitors\|P2Y12 inhibitors]]
Heparin drip 24h after
