**Secondary Hypogonadism**
Prolactin normally inhibits GnRH secretion resulting in less LH and FSH secretion from gonadotrophs in the anterior pituitary
Consider: prolactinoma

[[klinefelter syndrome\|klinefelter syndrome]] presents with increased LH and FSH due to **Primary Hypogonadism**
