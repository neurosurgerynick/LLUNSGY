## Fusion
- bone graft and instrumentation to ensure the fusion takes place between the two vertebral bodies

## Anatomy of the lumbar spine
There are 5 lumbar segments, and the weight-bearing part is anterior [[Denis 3 column classification\|Denis 3 column classification]]
The pedicles and the laminalextens posteriorly to form the arch
**Supraspinous ligament is the strongest ligament** is the strongest ligament of the spine
	When you do a laminectomy, you want to stay away from midline and not violate the supraspinous ligament so that you don't create iatrogenic instability. Then you can perform the laminectomy unilaterally.

## Spondylolysis types
1. **Ishmic** is a fracture of the pars interarticularis, a stress fracture in the pars interarticularis that is common in young gymnasts
2. Congential/Dysplastic: secondary to pars interarticularis dysplasia
3. Pars interarticularis Spondylolysis: pars interarticularis is completely absent

## Grafts
1. Autograft: from the patient's iliac crest
	1. osteogenic (growth of bone cells), osteo**inductive** (BMPs (*recombinant human rhBMP2*)and molecular signals), and osteoconductive (scaffold for new blood vessels, *ceramic graft* generally considered safe)
2. Allograft: from a different person, cadaveric. Small risk of transmission of a blood-borne illness, but those risks are low
3. Synthetic: manmade
