#### Square formed between the borders of:
- superior facet
- inferior facet
- lateral border of lateral mass
- medial border of lateral mass
![[Pasted image 20231207064848.png \| 400]]
Screw enters **Inferomedial**, penetrate cortex with a thin burr
![Pasted image 20231207065003.png](/img/user/assets/Pasted%20image%2020231207065003.png)
Aim the screws 25 degrees laterally to avoid the vertebral arteries which are medial
![Pasted image 20231207065203.png](/img/user/assets/Pasted%20image%2020231207065203.png)
