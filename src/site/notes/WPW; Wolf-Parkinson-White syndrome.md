Diagnosis: shortened PT interval and slurred upstroke of the QRS called a delta wave
Next best step: **electrophysiology study**
Reason for passing out: **tachyarrhythmia** due to the accessory pathway bypassing the AV node (faster than the AV node) directly to the ventricles
WPW syndrome is when you have WPW pattern, syncope, and documented SVT (note that afib can degenerate into vfib): Tx is **catheter ablation**
#### You cannot terminate arrhythmias in patients with AV nodal blocking agents, as this will precipitate a ventricular arrhythmia and death therefore the drug of choice is **Procainamide!**
