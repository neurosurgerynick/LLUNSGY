## Metopic craniosynostosis, Trigonocephaly
Always see the patient, get a 3D reconstruction on the CT for presurgical planning
best time to perform surgery is between 6-12 months for cranial vault remodeling
metopic suture closes at 3-6 months
metopic craniosynostosis causes trigonocephaly
no minimally invasive procedure is able to fix the metopic suture "disaster"
non syndromic craniosynostosis, metopic and coronal has the highest rates of orbital issues
Apert syndrome?
Crouzon syndrome?
bifrontal craniotomy with frontoorbital advancement to the superior orbital ridge
significant risk of developmental delay due to brain compression, surgery is still offered
plagiocephaly vs. lambdoid craniosynostosis: plagiocephaly the ears are forward, lambdoid everything is anchored back, so the ears are posterior. best way to look at the patient is from the top
