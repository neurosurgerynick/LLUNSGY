Oral succimer can be used as a chelating agent in lead poisoning, which can present with abdominal pain, vomiting, and lethargy.  However, anion gap metabolic acidosis is not seen.
	Side effects of succimer: rash, neutropenia
Severe lead posioning: >70 ug/dL
	Tx: IM dimercaprol followed by admission and IV EDTA
