## Pre-surgical Conference
Case of 68 year old woman:
Findings: 
- M1 occlusion, no superior MCA division, and enlarged anterior temporal artery (acquired from atherosclerotic disease)
- ACA to MCA leptomeningeal (pial) collaterals are supplying the superior division of the MCA off to the right side
- Left vertebral artery ends in the PICA, occluded at its origin into the basilar artery
- Right dominant vertebral artery
- Variants Present: Duplicated SCA, and opthalmic origin of the MMA

### Case 2
82 year old male with a parietal based lesion, hyperintense on T1, patchy contrast enhancement
Took him for a stereotactic biopsy and it came back as high grade glioma
Recall that for high grade glioma surgery when offering surgery the two most important things that help with your decision making are patient age, and KPS

Operative considerations:
Neuronavigation, Neuromonitoring, EMG, MEP with phase reversal to map the central sulcus, subcortical MEP


> Look up MR Spectroscopy findings: tumor vs. infection


## Milestone Didactics

Make sure in the operative note you are writing IPATOS; infection present at time of surgery
If there is pus, the wound is dirty
